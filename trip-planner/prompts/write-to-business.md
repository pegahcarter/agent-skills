# Write to a business

Draft only. Never send.

## Shape

- Local language first, English translation beside or below it, so the user
  can check what is being asked.
- Formal register.
- One line of context: who you are, the dates, the party size.
- At most three questions, each specific and answerable in one line. Ask for
  the operational facts the plan depends on, not general information.
- Sign with the name or initials in `config.yaml` (`sign_off`).

## Good questions

- "Are you open to visitors on Tuesday 22 September between 14:00 and 16:00?"
- "Can we buy directly without a tasting, or is a booking required?"
- "Is the gym available before 06:00 on request?"

## Weak questions

- "What are your hours?" (ask about the exact date and window instead)
- "Tell us about your wines." (it is on their site)
- More than three questions (replies answer the first and skip the rest)

## After a reply

Update the tracker with the answer and its source ("email reply 2026-09-03"),
clear the yellow fill on confirmed fields, change Status, and tick the
matching Open decisions line in the itinerary.
